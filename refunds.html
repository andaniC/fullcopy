Refund policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

14 days, in full. If Full Copy Pro isn’t right for you, email us within 14 days of any payment and we’ll refund that payment. No forms, and you don’t need to give a reason.

How to ask

Email mukwevhoandani1020@gmail.com from the address you paid with, or include your order number from the receipt.

What happens next

  • The refund goes back to the card or account you paid with, through Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider. It can take up to 10 days to show on your statement.
  • Refunding a payment also cancels the subscription, so you won’t be charged again, and Pro switches off in your browsers within a day.
  • Your saved copies stay on your computer.

After 14 days

You can cancel any time to stop future payments. Pro keeps working until the end of the month you’ve paid for. If Full Copy stopped working for you and we couldn’t fix it, write to us anyway and we’ll sort it out.

Before you pay

Full Copy Pro starts with a 7-day free trial, so you can try it on your own work first. Cancel before the 7 days are up and nothing is charged.
