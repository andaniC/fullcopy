What Full Copy does

Normally, copying from a website leaves the pictures behind, and AI chats like ChatGPT drop them when you paste. Full Copy keeps them, whether it’s a support ticket with its screenshots, a chart from a report or a diagram for your notes.

  • Copy with the pictures

    Select text and pictures on any website and copy as usual (Ctrl+C, or ⌘C on a Mac). Paste into Google Docs, Notion, Word or an email and the pictures are there too.

  • Maths that AI can read

    Copying an equation usually gives a jumble like “a2+b2=c2”. Full Copy turns it into LaTeX, the standard way to type maths, so ChatGPT and Claude read it exactly as written.

  • Pictures in AI chats

    When you paste into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, the text goes in and every picture is attached, in order. Works in Slack, Discord and WhatsApp Web too.

  • Collect from several pages

    Pulling things together from different places? Right-click and choose “Add to collection” on each page you need, then paste it all in one go.

  • Your recent copies

    Click the Full Copy icon in Chrome to see your last 60 copies and copy any of them again.

  • Private

    No account and no tracking. What you copy stays on your computer. Privacy policy
